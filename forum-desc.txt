[CENTER][SIZE=7][B]F95Zone Ultimate Enhancer[/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=4]A configurable userscript that improves F95Zone's Latest Updates and thread pages, with optional add-ons for specialized tools.[/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=5][B]Features[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*]Latest Updates overlays, scoring, layouts, refresh controls, and recovery
[*]Thread overlays, wide layouts, and collapsible signatures
[*]Preferred, excluded, and marked tag management with custom colors
[*]Isolated settings, configuration transfer, and Feature Health diagnostics
[*]Optional Library, Latest Filters, Thread Utility, Site Repair, and Masked + Direct Download add-ons
[/LIST]
