# Masked Direct standalone expansion baseline

This records `MASKED-DIRECT-STANDALONE-EXPANSION-BASELINE-01`. It is
characterization only and does not authorize another host or change source,
manifest metadata, versions, catalogs, or distributions.

## Evidence

- Route fixtures live in
  `tests/fixtures/masked-direct/standalone-expansion-routes.json`.
- Machine-readable side-effect ownership lives in
  `masked-direct-standalone-expansion-baseline.json`.
- The focused test group proves all proposed expansion entries remain blocked
  today while the five existing approved standalone families remain unchanged.
- The same group proves a fresh exact managed request still wins for an
  expansion route without consulting standalone eligibility.

## Operation classes

- Single-page: Buzzheavier/Bzzhr, Gofile, MediaFire.
- Single-page but staged or DOM-qualified: MixDrop and UploadNow.
- Continuation ownership required: Google Drive confirmation redirects,
  Datanodes stages, Vik1ngFile/VikingFile stages, and Workupload `/start/*`.

Known aliases are exact. The reported `miixdrop.com` route is retained as
observed-but-unmatched until its manifest and metadata support are explicitly
verified in a later package.

## Retained boundary

Route recognition is only one authorization input. A managed request remains
authoritative. Requestless automation additionally requires effective
standalone policy and an approved route. Continuation pages cannot establish
their own ownership merely because a handler could find a clickable element.
